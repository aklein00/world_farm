WORLD FARM STUDIO

One style.
A family of buildings.

Choose broad style inputs, approve a constrained recipe and test the look with one cube-built sample. Reuse that style for buildings and decorations, then assemble a small world.

Open studio ↗Explore the workflow ↓

The studio is a working prototype; explicit controls generate cube recipes locally, without AI or credits. Short text is saved as direction notes, not interpreted by a model. Projects are saved in your browser. Sign in to open the studio.

THE IDEA

A shared style,
not a fixed building.

Keep palette, windows, doors, roofs and trim coordinated without making every building the same.

Input
Style family + building brief
Working set
Buildings · decorations · terrain
Output
Editable project · GLB · PNG

Designed for look development, composition studies and reusable environment kits.

THE JOURNEY

From a style family to a little world.

Style inputs → prompt approval → sample approval → assets → world → review. Your existing projects remain available in the previous studio.

  1. 01 →

    Choose a broad style

    Start with Botanicals, Terraced City-States or Retro-Future, or choose a theme. Set shape language, proportions and palette.

  2. 02 →

    Approve the constrained prompt

    Review the organized direction and locked cube-geometry rules. Create one sample, then approve the style or return to the inputs.

  3. 03 →

    Create a reusable asset

    Choose a building or decoration and bounded dimensions. Review the cube preview and save its recipe with the approved style.

  4. 04 →

    Assemble a small world

    Place and rotate saved assets on a 4 × 4 grid. Preview stepped plot elevations and library replacements before applying them.

  5. 05

    Review and export

    Check silhouette, color and scale. Export a reopenable project, cube-model GLB or current-view PNG.
